## Full Description
Riento helps you decide where to go tonight in Helsinki.

Explore bars and clubs on a dark map, compare venue rankings, check broad crowd labels such as Quiet, Active, Busy, and Packed, and view venue-level now-playing information when available.

Use Riento to:
- Browse Helsinki nightlife without creating an account.
- Sort nearby venues with foreground-only location.
- Save My spots and preferences locally.
- Save account-free 1-5 feedback privately, use an optional one-tap foreground venue/song check with private Yes/No feedback, and contribute k=2 venue-level crowd and trusted Android music signals.
- Optionally use email or Google to add pre-moderated written reviews.
- Export or delete Riento data directly in Settings.

Riento does not display individual user locations, account emails/provider identifiers, private app identifiers, or exact public crowd counts. Venue and live-signal information can be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate.
