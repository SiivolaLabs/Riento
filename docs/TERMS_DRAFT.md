# Riento Terms Of Service Draft

Status: draft for final legal review before public launch.

## Summary
Riento helps users discover where nightlife venues appear active. Crowd and music signals are estimates, not guaranteed real-time facts.

## Eligibility
Users should only use Riento for nightlife decisions if they are legally allowed to enter relevant venues and use age-restricted services in their location.

## Optional Review Accounts
Riento does not require an account for browsing, ratings, crowd signals, music signals, reports, My spots, or settings. A random pseudonymous backend session is created only when needed to authorize an account-free contribution, apply cooldowns, or provide data controls. Users may optionally use email/password, Google, or Apple to add written venue reviews. Pilot email accounts activate without inbox-ownership confirmation, so users must provide an address they control for password recovery. My spots and settings remain local and are not recoverable or transferable through Riento.

## Acceptable Use
Users must not:
- Submit knowingly false venue, crowd, rating, report, or music information
- Try to identify, track, or harass other people
- Evade cooldowns, create sessions in bulk, or manipulate public venue status
- Interfere with the app, backend, matching providers, or venue data
- Use Riento for illegal, unsafe, or abusive activity
- Post spam, threats, harassment, hate, sexual exploitation, personal information, impersonation, or other objectionable written-review content

## Crowd And Music Estimates
Two recent private identities may publish community Active, while Busy/Packed require an authorized temporary estimate. Trusted Android provider metadata or an authorized admin's manually verified song may update now-playing; iPhone matches and user Yes/No confirmations remain private. Accuracy feedback does not automatically publish, remove, or correct a song. These signals can be wrong, delayed, unavailable, or stale. Exact public crowd counts and individual locations are not shown.

## Ratings, Written Reviews, And Reports
Number-only venue ratings do not require an account and are saved as private feedback. Public summaries require a minimum sample of permanent-account ratings. Written reviews require an optional account and are held for moderation before publication; attached ratings remain private until text is approved or removed. Users can report approved reviews and block review authors. Riento may approve, reject, hide, remove, or preserve moderation records for abusive, fraudulent, inaccurate, off-topic, or spam submissions. Review accounts and their attributable data can be deleted in Settings.

## Venue Data
Names, addresses, coordinates, categories, opening hours, status, ratings, and signals may be wrong or outdated. Users remain responsible for confirming venue availability, entry rules, and suitability.

## Safety
Users remain responsible for their plans, transportation, health, safety, and legal compliance. Riento is not an emergency service.
