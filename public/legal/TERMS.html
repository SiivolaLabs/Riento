Eligibility

Use Riento only if you are legally allowed to enter relevant venues and use age-restricted services in your location.

Optional Review Accounts

Riento does not require an account for browsing, number ratings, crowd or music signals, reports, My spots, or settings. A random pseudonymous backend session authorizes account-free contributions. Users may optionally use email/password, Google, or Apple to add written venue reviews. Pilot email accounts activate without inbox-ownership confirmation, so users must provide an address they control for password recovery. My spots and settings remain local and are not transferable through Riento.

Crowd, Ratings, Reports, And Music

Community crowd activity requires more than one recent private identity, and community submissions cannot create Busy or Packed during launch. Number-only ratings need no account but remain private unless they come from established accounts and the public minimum sample is met. Written reviews require an optional account and are held for moderation before publication. Android provider matches and authorized admins' manually verified songs may publish through trusted server code; iPhone ShazamKit matches and user Yes/No feedback remain private. Accuracy feedback does not automatically publish, remove, or correct a song. Users can report reviews and block review authors. Riento may approve, reject, hide, limit, or remove abusive, fraudulent, inaccurate, off-topic, or spam submissions.
