Riento Community Guidelines

Effective date: 2026-07-21

Written reviews should help people understand a venue. Keep reviews based on genuine experience, specific to the venue, and respectful.

Not Allowed

  • Threats, harassment, hate, or targeted abuse
  • Sexual exploitation or content involving minors
  • Personal information, doxxing, or attempts to identify another person
  • Impersonation, fraud, undisclosed promotion, or spam
  • Knowingly false claims or content unrelated to the venue
  • Instructions for illegal or dangerous conduct

Moderation And Safety

New and edited written reviews are private until approved. Riento may reject, hide, or remove content and may restrict abusive accounts. Users can report a published review and block its author from the venue page.

For safety or moderation concerns, email Siivola.Labs@proton.me with the venue and review date. Do not include unnecessary personal information.
